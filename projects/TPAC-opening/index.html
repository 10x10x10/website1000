臺北表演藝術中心開幕主視覺

TPAC Opening Visual Design

位於士林的臺北表演藝術中心，為迎接正式開幕，推出整季的開幕展演活動。北藝中心建築本體極具現代感視覺張力，因此主視覺以直拳方式直接地將北藝中心的兩大建築元素（球體、波浪）進行平面化解構，球體以金屬酸性視覺（Acid Graphics）、波浪牆以錯視（Optical illusion）等視覺效果，作為這次主視覺的呈現方式。

To celebrate its official opening, the Taipei Performing Arts Center in Shilin presented a season-long programme of opening performances and events. As the building itself is strikingly modern, the key visual takes a direct approach, deconstructing its two main architectural elements, the sphere and the wave, into flat graphics. The sphere is rendered with metallic acid graphics and the wave wall with optical illusion effects, together forming the key visual for the opening season.
